A potion shop you run with your hands

Every potion is made from scratch: no menus, no auto-craft.

🌿

Prepare ingredients

Chop herbs in rhythm, cut roots and mushrooms, fry and flip, grind in a mortar, pound each ingredient with its own force.

🔥

Brew in the cauldron

Work the bellows with one thumb and stir with the other. Harder potions need different heat at every stage.

🧙

Serve your customers

Peasants, knights, bards, merchants, witches, mages and rich patrons who buy two at once, each with their own patience.

✨

Finish at the counter

Pour to the line, then shake, add essence drops, sprinkle powder, warm it up, or say a magic word over it.

🔮

Find the secrets

Experiment in the lab. Three secret potions are in no recipe book.

🏆

Grow the shop

Bigger cauldrons, a juice press, lucky talismans, new recipes and achievements.
