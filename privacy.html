Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Potioncrafter (Ukrainian: Зіллярня) is a single-player game for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it sends only anonymous gameplay statistics so we can make the game better.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, for example how far players get, which potions they brew and whether the difficulty feels right. It records:

  • game events, such as finishing a day, reaching a level, brewing a potion or unlocking an achievement;
  • in-app purchases (which product was bought; never your name or payment details);
  • basic technical information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate country or region worked out from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored);
  • a random ID that Firebase creates for this installation of the game, so visits can be counted. It is not your Apple ID or the advertising identifier, and it resets if you delete the app.

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account, it is not used for advertising, and it is not sold or shared to track you across other apps and websites. Google processes it for us under the Firebase Data Processing Terms; see Google's Privacy Policy. Statistics are kept for up to 14 months.

We do not use the advertising identifier, and the game never asks for permission to track you.

Your game progress

Your coins, recipes, equipment and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

Some potions end with an optional magic word. Only if you choose to speak it does the game ask for microphone and speech recognition access. Your voice is recognized by Apple's speech service: on your device when it supports this, otherwise through Apple's servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game does not record, store or send your audio to us. Without permission, you simply pick the word from three choices.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can unlock it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and the statistics above are anonymous.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the statistics for your installation by writing to the address on the Support page. Deleting the app also resets its random ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
