Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Potioncrafter (Ukrainian: Зіллярня) is a single-player game for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and we do not use the advertising identifier.

Your game progress

Your coins, recipes, equipment and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

Some potions end with an optional magic word. Only if you choose to speak it does the game ask for microphone and speech recognition access. Your voice is recognized by Apple's speech service: on your device when it supports this, otherwise through Apple's servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game does not record, store or send your audio to us. Without permission, you simply pick the word from three choices.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can unlock it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if the game ever adds anonymous usage statistics, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
