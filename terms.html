The first two levels are free. The Full Version is a one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the game on every device signed in to the same Apple ID. Use “Restore Purchases” in the game to unlock it again after reinstalling or on a new device.

Coins

Coins are an in-game currency with no real-world value. They cannot be exchanged for money or transferred, and they are stored only on your device: deleting the app or erasing your progress deletes them. Bought coin packs are consumed when added to your game. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing.
