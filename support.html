I bought the full version, but it's locked again

Open the coin shop (the “+” next to your coins at the top) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

My coins or progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on your device, so it is lost if the app is deleted. Bought coins can't be restored after that, but the Full Version can.

The magic word doesn't hear me

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Potioncrafter in the iPhone Settings app, or just pick the word from the three choices instead.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your iPhone's language: Ukrainian if your iPhone is set to Ukrainian, English otherwise. To choose just for this game: Settings → Apps → Potioncrafter → Language.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
